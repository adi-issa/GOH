Who We Are

Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

We exist so that no Palestinian family in South Australia stands alone — keeping our culture alive, our people connected, and our identity proud.

01

Our Mission

To bring Palestinians together and celebrate our culture, heritage and history with one another as well as the broader Australian community and maintain a positive identity among the greater public.

02

Our Vision

Be the go-to organisation for residents, citizens and new comers of Palestinian origins, to connect, meet and support each other and accelerate integrating into society while keeping close ties with our community, heritage and history.

03

Our Values

  • Respect: At the core of everything we do is respect. Respect for one another. Respect for our ideas. Respect for our culture.
  • Integrity: Our promise is to be open and honest with everything we do. We pride ourselves in behaving righteously and putting our community's best interests first.
  • Unity: Building a strong and united community is essential for the success of our organisation. For we are only as successful as we are united.
  • Empathy: We share in each of our successes as we also share one another's pain. For that we care deeply for each and every member in our community to ensure that we help one another in our lows and support one another in our highs.
  • Growth: We want to grow the Palestinian community that highlights our heritage and represents us in a way that makes us proud.
What We Set Out To Do

Our Objectives

01

Promote & disseminate information about Palestinian history, heritage and culture in Australia.

02

Provide a forum for Palestinian Australians to practice their culture, celebrate their heritage and to foster closer ties between them.

03

Encourage and promote Palestinian community's integration with the Australian society and its diverse ethnic groups.

04

Engage and collaborate with similar organisations that support the same objectives.

05

Raise funds for charitable causes including the support of new arrivals and refugees.

Our Story

A decade of building belonging

Glimmer of Hope began in 2016 with a simple idea: that no Palestinian family arriving in South Australia should feel alone. What started as a handful of families sharing meals and helping newcomers find their feet has grown into one of the state's most active Palestinian community organisations.

Today we run cultural events, youth and language programs, and practical settlement support — all powered by volunteers who give their time so the next generation grows up proud of who they are.

Built by the community, for the community.

Our Roots

Palestine Heritage

Palestine is a land of ancient civilisations, rich traditions and unbroken spirit. Our community carries that heritage proudly into every event, every conversation, and every act of solidarity in South Australia.

Villages & Homeland

Hundreds of Palestinian villages and cities form the tapestry of our ancestral homeland — from Haifa and Jaffa to Hebron, Nablus, Jerusalem and beyond.

Dabkeh (Traditional Palestinian Dance)

Dabkeh is the heartbeat of Palestinian folk culture — a powerful collective dance performed at weddings, celebrations, and cultural events across the Levant.

Tatreez Embroidery

Palestinian tatreez (embroidery) is a UNESCO-recognised art form. Each geometric pattern tells the story of a village, a family, and a people's resilience.

Oral History

Stories passed from grandparents to grandchildren keep memory alive. GOH collects and preserves community oral histories for future generations.

Arabic Language

Language is identity. GOH runs Arabic classes for children and adults to ensure the next generation maintains their linguistic and cultural connection to Palestine.

Food & Cuisine

Palestinian cuisine — musakhan, maqluba, knafeh, za'atar and more — is an expression of love for the land and for one another, shared at every community gathering.

Settlement Services

Newcomer Essentials

Before You Arrive

Planning your move to South Australia? Our Welcome Committee can answer your questions.

Centrelink / Services Australia

Financial support for eligible newcomers. GOH volunteers can accompany you to your first appointment and help with Arabic translation.

servicesaustralia.gov.au

Medicare Enrolment

Register for Medicare to access Australia's public healthcare system. We guide you through enrolment at your nearest Service Centre.

Medicare enrolment guide

Housing Support

GOH maintains community contacts and rental guidance for northern Adelaide suburbs. SA Housing Authority assists with social housing applications.

housing.sa.gov.au

School Enrolment

GOH helps enrol children in local public schools and connects families with ESL support programs for a smooth transition.

education.sa.gov.au

AMEP English Classes

The Adult Migrant English Program offers free English lessons for eligible migrants. GOH helps community members enrol and navigate class schedules.

amepaustralia.org.au

SA Drivers Licence

Guidance on converting your overseas licence to a South Australian licence, including test preparation and Service SA appointment bookings.

sa.gov.au — Licences
